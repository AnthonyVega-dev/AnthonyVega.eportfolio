Core Value 1

Collaboration & Impact

Why This Matters to Me

I believe the most meaningful outcomes are never achieved alone. Every organization, team, and community I've been part of has reinforced that collaboration is the engine behind real impact.

From leading chapters to working alongside professors to recruiting new members, I've seen firsthand how bringing people together with a shared goal creates results none of us could achieve individually.

How I Live This Value

I actively seek out collaborative environments, contribute with intention, and always keep the bigger picture — the impact on people and community — at the center of what I do.

Core Value 2

Innovation With Purpose

Why This Matters to Me

Innovation isn't just about technology or disruption — it's about finding better, more thoughtful ways to solve human problems. In HR, that means rethinking how we recruit, develop, and support people.

During my time at Delta Sigma Pi, I implemented document changes and back-end efficiencies that improved operations for 140+ members. That's innovation with purpose — practical, people-focused improvement.

How I Live This Value

I constantly ask "how can this be better?" — whether in a process, a program, or an interaction. I bring fresh thinking to every role I take on while staying grounded in what actually serves people best.

Core Value 3

People-Centered Leadership

Why This Matters to Me

Leadership is not a title — it's a responsibility. I've served as a Member, Vice President, and President of the Heralds, and each role taught me that the best leaders put others' growth before their own recognition.

As VP of Chapter Operations at Delta Sigma Pi, I managed operations for 140+ members and conducted 20 structured interviews. Leadership at that scale demands empathy, structure, and genuine care for people.

How I Live This Value

I lead by listening first. I create environments where people feel seen and supported, and I measure my success as a leader by how much those around me are growing — not just by what I personally accomplish.

Core Value 4

Growth Mindset

Why This Matters to Me

I started college as a member of a public speaking club. I became its President. I joined a business fraternity, then led its operations. I got a 3.7 GPA while working, leading, and building. That's what a growth mindset looks like in practice.

I'm currently studying for the SHRM-CP exam because I don't want to wait until after graduation to be taken seriously as an HR professional. Growth, for me, is always intentional — never accidental.

How I Live This Value

I seek out challenges that stretch me, ask for feedback even when it's uncomfortable, and invest in my development through certifications, coursework, and experiences that push me beyond where I already am.
